What the colours mean

SafeRiver is at its usual level. Nothing to do.
Be alertMore water than usual. Keep watching for news.
Get readyWater is high. Pack your bag and plan where to go.
DangerWater may enter homes. Move to safety when told.

Dam warnings

Blue warningThe dam is filling up. No need to worry yet.
Orange warningThe dam is nearly full. Water may be let out soon. People by the river should get ready.
Red warningWater will be let out. Follow instructions to move away from the river.

Keep these in one bag

If you live near a river, keep a bag packed during the monsoon so you can leave quickly.

💧Drinking water
🍞Dry food
💊Medicines
📄ID & papers in a plastic cover
🔦Torch
🔋Charged phone & power bank
👕Spare clothes
🧴Soap & sanitiser

✅ Do

  • Move old people, children and animals early
  • Switch off electricity before leaving
  • Listen to official announcements
  • Go to the nearest relief camp if asked

❌ Don't

  • Walk or drive through flowing water
  • Go to the river to watch or take photos
  • Believe or forward unverified messages
  • Wait until water enters your house
